## Conventions

- Every claim traces to a specific item. Numbers trace to where the number is stated.
- Disagreements are marked, not resolved silently.
- A correction propagates to every file carrying the claim, in the same pass.
- Sections are versioned, not edited invisibly. Record what changed and when.
